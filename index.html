🗺️ How to find each collectible

Choose a marker category above, zoom in, then tap a pin on the map. The external map may show images, screenshots, comments or collection tips for individual pins, depending on what its contributors have added.

📦ChestsCheck rooftops, caves, cliffs, hidden rooms, and puzzle rewards. Some require defeating enemies or completing challenges.
💎OculiCheck height: above buildings, underground, floating in the air, or inside caves. Use the pin details to find the exact level.
🧩PuzzlesLook for nearby mechanisms, elemental monuments, torches, pressure plates and region-specific mechanics.
✨Seelies & trialsFollow Seelies to their courts and activate timed challenges to reveal their rewards.
📜QuestsSome hidden areas and chests are locked behind world quests or exploration objectives.
🌀WaypointsLook above and below ground; some teleport points unlock only after progressing through quests.

These are general tips, not verified instructions or images for every individual pin. Exact location screenshots depend on the external map provider.

Upload an Android screen recording of your Genshin map. The tracker samples frames and reads visible exploration percentages. Review all matches before saving.
