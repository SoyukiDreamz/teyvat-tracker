☾ Teyvat Tips & Tricks ♡

Smart ways to earn Primogems, explore faster, and prepare your characters — without cheats, account-sharing, or risky shortcuts.

💎 Primogems · Daily

Do your Daily Commissions

Complete your daily commission rewards or use Adventure Encounters when available. Claim your rewards from Katheryne when required. Small daily gains add up!

💎 Primogems · Events

Check limited-time events first

Events often give Primogems and have deadlines. Open the Events menu and prioritize rewards that expire soon.

💎 Primogems · Exploration

Open chests and finish puzzles

Explore unexplored areas, solve puzzles, complete time trials, and unlock achievements. Primogem rewards vary by chest and location.

💎 Primogems · Quests

Finish quests and unlock areas

Archon, World, Story, and Hangout content can unlock new areas, achievements, and rewards. Check each quest's rewards before prioritizing it.

💎 Primogems · Challenges

Try the Spiral Abyss and other endgame challenges

Claim the rewards you can comfortably earn. Availability and reset schedules vary by mode and game version.

💎 Primogems · Free rewards

Check official announcements

Redeem active official codes promptly and check in-game mail, maintenance compensation, and update announcements. Never enter your login on unofficial reward sites.

🗺️ Exploration

Unlock teleport waypoints early

Activate waypoints and Statues of The Seven while traveling. They make later farming and quest routes much faster.

🗺️ Exploration

Search in small sections

Clear one area at a time, check cliffs and underground passages, and mark discoveries on your tracker to avoid revisiting the same spots.

🗺️ Exploration

Use Treasure Compasses

Unlock regional Treasure Compasses through the relevant progression systems. They help locate nearby eligible chests but do not reveal every puzzle or reward.

🗺️ Exploration

Collect Oculi along the way

Mark Oculi you find and offer them to Statues of The Seven. Use regional Resonance Stones when available to locate missing ones.

⚔️ Characters

Spend Original Resin intentionally

Plan boss materials, talent books, weapon materials, and artifacts around the characters you actually use.

⚔️ Characters

Upgrade guaranteed power first

Character levels, weapons, and relevant talents often provide more predictable improvements than repeatedly farming random artifacts.
